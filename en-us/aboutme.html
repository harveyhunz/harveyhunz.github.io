My journey with snooker has taught me that the game is far more than just potting balls—it is an art of deep focus, precision, and relentless self-refinement.

As a self-taught player moving from the basics to advanced cueing and position play, I understand firsthand how challenging it can be to improve without a dedicated coach. I built this app to integrate all the essential tools I wished I had along the way—from match scoring to structured error analysis and training logs. My goal is to support fellow players who share the same passion, helping you practice smarter and track every bit of your progress.

My ultimate dream is to compete on the professional international stage and see just how far this dedication can take me. If this app brings value to your game, or if you’d like to back my journey and training, any support—even just the price of a coffee—means the world to me.

Thank you for being part of this journey. See you at the table!
